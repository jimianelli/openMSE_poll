Updated pollock inputs: what is ready

Published

October 9, 2026

Annual assessment files

The pm_25.dat candidate provides an additional model year, fishery composition, BTS information, and AVO observation. Its annual weight matrices fail the intake check: 62 rows are required; fishery weights have 61 and spawning weights have 63. Reconcile their calendar-year mapping in the source project. Truncation, repeated terminal weights, or a shift of the row-year alignment would be new scientific assumptions and must be specified before fitting.

The pm_26.dat candidate ends in 2024. The field-by-field comparison finds identical values for all 53 core fields shared with the RDS cache; the final test field differs in shape. This is a legacy input snapshot, not a completed 2026 update. The older parser also treats year annotations following comment names as part of the field name. The audit parser extracts the first header token and checks row widths, repeated headers, and numeric values.

The 2026 BTS workbook

The named sheet bts_abundance_at_age_1982_2026_ contains ages 1–14 and an explicit 15+ column. The 2024, 2025, and 2026 rows each have 15 observed cells. Numerical year filtering excludes trailing scratch calculations labelled Correc and blank rows. The audit retains missing survey years as missing observations.

The Index and AgeComps products stop in 2024 and include EBS and NBS strata. The BTS Natage sheet fails with a missing rId3 workbook relationship. Mixed-type warnings from the older VAST_ddc_all_2024 CPUE sheet are recorded in workbook warnings; that sheet is not used for the current abundance summary. Update biomass indices and uncertainties from an identified current product, rather than inferring them from the abundance sheet.

Survey weight-at-age

The reviewed VAST-derived weight file covers 1982–2026 with 675 year–age cells, 657 observed weight/SE pairs, and 18 paired missing cells. All 15 cells in 2026 are observed. Units are kg per fish and kg for SE; the stored cv is a ratio, which is multiplied by 100 for a percentage. These are model-derived survey weights; adoption as final assessment inputs remains unverified.

Fishery weights and a coherent annual assessment input package are still required. Keep BTS and fishery schedules distinct. The terminal age must continue to mean 15+. Missing survey cells must follow the chosen model’s observation rules.

Model 26.0 reference evidence

Six diagnostic/configuration CSVs were checked against their live counterparts on the published September 2026 assessment development site. Five of six differ from the local working copies. The public tables describe a 1964–2024 development fit, Rceattle 5.23.0, source commit adf22f84b399e84e3b9a707e0b3bbb1624179a27. This designation describes model development and does not establish adoption for current advice.

The point estimate has maximum gradient 4.984 × 10^-5 and a positive-definite Hessian. The retrospective terminal years 2016 and 2019 exceed this project’s strict 10^-4 gradient criterion despite their recorded OK status. The posterior table reports maximum R-hat 1.075, minimum bulk ESS 36.0, and minimum tail ESS 21.7; its own targets are R-hat ≤ 1.01 and ESS ≥ 400. Resolve mixing and effective sample size before importing draws into the OM. The published projection configuration uses F40% and F35% in a Tier 3 development check. It explicitly retains the accepted assessment for management advice.

The newer local working exports have a different objective and diagnostic profile. Their source fingerprints are recorded alongside the live hashes. These versions must be reconciled to one designated run before an assessment-to-OM export. The local working results also exceed their posterior diagnostic targets.
